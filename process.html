Deeper Look

Inside each step.

01

Discovery Call

The call is intentionally short. In twenty minutes we cover your goals, your timeline, your current credit profile at a high level, and the use case for the capital. By the end of the call, both sides know whether there's a fit worth pursuing.

What you walk away with: A candid read on expected facility size, likely timeline, and any profile items worth addressing before we submit.
02

Soft-Pull Review

Before anything touches your credit report, we run a soft-pull analysis, pulling the same data lenders see, without any of the scoring impact. We model the specific lenders most likely to approve you, the total facility size you can expect, and any optimizations worth making first.

What you walk away with: A written modeled facility range, a lender-by-lender strategy, and any profile cleanup items with concrete next steps.
03

Strategic Build-Out

This is where our specialty shows up. Rather than submitting a single application, we build a coordinated stack across our lender partners, sequenced and timed to maximize approvals and minimize credit impact. You handle the signatures; we handle everything else.

What you walk away with: A fully submitted, actively-managed application stack and a weekly update cadence until every line lands.
04

Funded & Deployed

As each line is approved, we coordinate funding, activation, and any balance transfers or draws. We walk through how to optimize interest-free periods, how to manage utilization for the long haul, and when to come back for your next facility.

What you walk away with: A funded, active credit facility and a playbook for running it over the next 12 to 24 months.
