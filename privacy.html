Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 9, 2026

Blue Jay Capital ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information, including information collected through SMS/text messaging communications.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including but not limited to:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Property, financial, and business-related information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Communicate with you regarding investment, real estate, and business opportunities
  • Provide advisory, brokerage, and consulting services
  • Send updates, notifications, and responses to inquiries
  • Deliver SMS/text messages related to your engagement with us

3. SMS/Text Messaging Policy

By providing your phone number, you consent to receive SMS/text messages from Blue Jay Capital related to your inquiry, transactions, or ongoing services.

  • Message frequency may vary
  • Message and data rates may apply
  • You can opt out at any time by replying STOP
  • For assistance, reply HELP or contact us directly

We only send messages that are relevant to your relationship with us.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information, including phone numbers or SMS consent, with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your information may only be shared with trusted partners strictly as necessary to deliver our services (e.g., escrow, legal, lending, or transaction-related parties).

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You may:

  • Opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP
  • Request access to or deletion of your personal information by contacting us

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact:

Blue Jay Capital
Email: mark@bluejay.capital

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with an updated effective date.
