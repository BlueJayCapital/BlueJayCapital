Our Story

About Blue Jay Capital

A boutique advisory built around one specialty: helping high net worth individuals access unsecured personal credit, with speed, discretion, and strategy.

Our Foundation

One focus, done exceptionally well.

Blue Jay Capital was founded on a simple idea: strong personal credit, high income, and a clean financial profile should open doors, not trigger paperwork. The banking world is still built to reward the borrower with the most collateral to pledge, but our clients don't want to pledge anything.

They want capital that lives outside their balance sheet, ready to deploy when the right opportunity appears. We built this practice to serve that exact need. Every relationship, every lender partnership, every process we run is aimed at one outcome: the largest possible unsecured credit facility, structured around your profile, funded in the shortest possible time.

Established Specialists,
not generalists.
Our Principles

What guides every engagement.

◆

Discretion

Your financial profile is confidential. Our engagements are private, and our process is designed to protect your credit report and your privacy at every step.

◆

Transparency

You'll always know exactly what we're doing, what it costs, and what you can expect. No moving targets, no surprise fees, no ambiguous timelines.

◆

Speed

Our clients don't have time for a 60-day process. We've refined our workflow to the point where funded capital in three weeks is the norm, not the exception.

◆

Depth

Unsecured credit is not a side business for us. It is the only thing we do, which is why we can out-structure and out-execute generalists every time.

◆

Partnership

We treat each engagement as a long-term relationship. Many of our clients return for additional facilities as their profile grows and their needs evolve.

◆

Integrity

If we can't structure a facility that serves your interests, we'll tell you upfront. We'd rather turn down a client than deliver a mediocre outcome.
